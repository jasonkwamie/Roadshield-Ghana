How it works

You see the price and the expected arrival time before anyone is on the way.

  1. Tell us what’s wrong

    Pick the problem and share your location on the map.

  2. A mechanic claims your job

    Verified mechanics nearby see your request and one takes it.

  3. Track the arrival

    Follow the mechanic live with an arrival countdown. You see a window of up to 4 hours.

  4. Pay in the app

    Pay with Mobile Money or card once the job is done.

Membership

Pay a monthly fee. Part of it becomes service credit that you can use on your jobs, and cheap callouts are free for members.

Silver

₵50 / month
  • ₵20 of every month’s fee becomes service credit
  • Free jump starts and basic diagnostics
  • Member rates on other jobs and on towing

Gold

₵120 / month
  • ₵48 of every month’s fee becomes service credit
  • Free jump starts and basic diagnostics
  • Free towing up to a set distance
  • Priority dispatch

Your credit adds up. Credit you don’t use builds from month to month, and you can put it towards a tow if you ever need one.

Questions

Where do you operate?

Accra only, during the pilot. We will grow from there once we have the service right.

How fast will someone arrive?

You see an arrival window of up to 4 hours, which allows for peak traffic. Most requests should be faster, and you can follow your mechanic on the map.

How much does it cost?

You see the price in the app before you confirm. Members get free callouts for cheap jobs and use their saved credit on bigger ones. Parts are paid for separately.

Do you tow cars?

We arrange towing through partner tow companies. You book and pay through RoadShield, so you get the member rate and can use saved credit.

Can I use RoadShield without a membership?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp and we will explain how non-member jobs work during the pilot.

I’m a mechanic. Can I join?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp. We verify every mechanic before they can take jobs.

Need help on the road, or want to join the pilot?
